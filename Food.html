Pav Bhaji

Pav bhaji is a world-famouse fast food or king of street food famouse in Maharashtra. In this, the vegetable are mixed with a unique blend of spices known as pav bhaji masala. It is served with soft bread or pav with a generous amount of butter topped with chopped onions and lemon juice.

Ingredients

  • Mixed vegetable
  • Green Peas
  • Pav Bhaji Masala
  • Butter
  • Pav

Step-by-Step process

  1. Boil vegetables:Pressure cook 2 potatoes, 1 cup cauliflower, ½ cup peas, 1 carrot, and 1 beetroot (small piece, for colour) until soft. Mash roughly.
  2. Sauté the base:Heat 2 tbsp butter and 1 tbsp oil in a pan. Add 1 chopped onion and cook until golden, then add 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste and 1 chopped capsicum.
  3. Add tomatoes:Stir in 3 chopped tomatoes and cook until mushy and the oil separates.
  4. Add spices:Mix in 2 tbsp pav bhaji masala, 1 tsp red chilli powder, ½ tsp turmeric, and salt to taste.
  5. Combine:Add the mashed vegetables with ½ cup water and mash everything together in the pan. Simmer for 10 minutes, adding more butter on top.
  6. Finish:Squeeze in lemon juice and sprinkle chopped coriander.
  7. Toast the pav:Slice the pav buns, toast them on a buttered tawa with a pinch of pav bhaji masala.
  8. Serve:Serve hot bhaji with buttered pav, chopped raw onion, a lemon wedge, and extra butter on top.
