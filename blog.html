5 Tips for study

Bhumi Rawat

Date:19 october 2026

Many students think that studying for more hours is the key to success. But the truth is that how you study matters more then how much you study.

  1. Make a timetable

    2. Pick a fixed time everyday. Being consistent is more important than studying for a long hour stretch in one day.

  2. Take short breaks

    3. After evry 25 minutes of studying, take a 5-minute break. This method is called the Pomodoro Technique.

  3. Keep your phone away

    4. While studying, put your phone on silent and keep it in another room. Notifications break your focus.

  4. Test yourself

    5. JUst reading is not enough. After studying, try to write down what you learned without looking at your notes

  5. Get enough sleep

    6. Warning:Staying up all night to study makes you remember less. Get 7 to 8 hours every day.

  6. In the end

    7. These tips are small, but if you follow them every day,you will definitely see a differemce. Take the first step today!
